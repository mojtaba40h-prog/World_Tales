The Pool of Tears

Chapter summary

Alice finds a cake marked “EAT ME” and grows into a giant, reaching nine feet tall. Overwhelmed by confusion and distress, she cries so intensely that her tears form a deep, salty pool around her feet. When the White Rabbit reappears and drops his gloves and fan in fear, Alice uses the fan and shrinks rapidly again.

Slipping into her own pool of tears, she swims alongside several strange creatures, including a Mouse, a Duck, and a Dodo. Desperate to reach dry land, Alice tries to converse politely with the Mouse, though she accidentally frightens the nervous animal by discussing her domestic cat.
