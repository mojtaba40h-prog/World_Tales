Chapter 8 of 12

The Queen’s Croquet-Ground

Chapter summary

Alice enters the royal garden and finds three gardeners painting white roses red to avoid being executed by the Queen of Hearts. Shortly after, the royal procession arrives, led by the King and Queen. The hot-tempered Queen demands executions for minor offenses, frequently shouting “Off with their head!”

She invites Alice to play a bizarre game of croquet using live flamingos as mallets, rolled-up hedgehogs as balls, and playing-card soldiers as arches. The game falls into complete chaos because the animals constantly move away. Suddenly, the floating head of the Cheshire Cat appears, causing an argument over how to execute a headless creature.
