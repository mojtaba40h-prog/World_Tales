Chapter 6 of 12

Pig and Pepper

Chapter summary

Alice arrives at a small house where a footman delivers an invitation to the Duchess from the Queen of Hearts. Inside, Alice discovers the Duchess holding a crying baby while the Cook prepares soup heavily seasoned with pepper. A broad-grinning Cheshire Cat sits nearby.

Unbothered by the chaotic, noisy room, the Duchess tosses the baby to Alice before leaving. Alice carries the child outside to save it, but the baby unexpectedly transforms into a small pig and trots away. Alice then meets the Cheshire Cat again in the forest, who directs her toward the March Hare and the Mad Hatter before fading into air.
