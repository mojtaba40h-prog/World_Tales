The Lobster Quadrille

Chapter summary

The Gryphon and the Mock Turtle happily demonstrate a lively dance called the Lobster Quadrille, describing how lobsters and sea animals dance together along the shore. They sing a silly song about a whiting and a snail while demonstrating the energetic steps. Afterwards, Alice recites a poem for them, but the words come out completely wrong, leaving her listeners confused and critical.

The Mock Turtle then sings a melancholic song dedicated to “Beautiful Soup.” Before he can finish his performance, a distant voice announces that a trial is beginning, prompting the Gryphon to grab Alice’s hand and rush her toward the courtroom.
