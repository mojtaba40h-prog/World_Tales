Chapter 2 of 7

The Law of Club and Fang

Chapter summary

Buck’s first day on the snowy beach of Dyea was a nightmare of confusion and violence. He found himself surrounded by savage husky dogs and rough men in a freezing, hostile climate. Here, civilized manners meant nothing.

Buck witnessed a shocking tragedy almost immediately when Curly, a friendly Newfoundland dog, attempted to approach a husky in a friendly manner. The husky attacked without warning, knocking Curly down while a circle of thirty other dogs rushed in to tear her to pieces. Buck realized that in this brutal environment, there was no fair play; once down, that was the end of you.

Buck was purchased by François and Perrault, two French-Canadian mail couriers working for the Canadian government. They placed Buck in a harness alongside experienced sled dogs, including Spitz, a fierce white husky who acted as the lead dog. Buck worked hard, quickly adapting to the demands of pulling heavy sleds over ice and snow.

Under the instruction of his harness mates and the watchful eye of François, he learned to stop, go, and navigate perilous mountain trails. To survive the freezing nights, Buck discovered the secret of sleeping buried under the snow to retain his body heat. His physical transformation was rapid: his muscles hardened like iron, his sight and smell grew sharper, and his digestive system adapted to scarce, rough food rations.

Primitive instincts, long buried under generations of civilized living, began to awaken within him, preparing him for the fierce struggles that lay ahead.
