The Dominant Primordial Beast

Chapter summary

The dominant primordial beast grew strong within Buck as the team pushed deeper into the frozen wilderness. A bitter rivalry quickly developed between Buck and Spitz, the ruthless lead dog. Spitz saw Buck as a dangerous rival who refused to show submission, while Buck openly challenged Spitz’s authority over the other dogs.

Tensions reached a boiling point during a cold night when a pack of starving wild huskies attacked their camp, causing chaos and injuring several dogs in the team. Despite the constant dangers of thin ice, freezing rivers, and exhaustion, the conflict between Buck and Spitz continued to intensify. Buck deliberately undermined Spitz’s leadership by protecting younger dogs when Spitz tried to punish them.

The discipline of the team began to break down, leading to frequent fights among the dogs. François noticed the growing hostility and knew that a final, deadly duel for mastership was inevitable. The decisive clash occurred late one night under the cold glow of the northern lights, after the team chased a snowshoe rabbit through the snow.

Spitz caught the rabbit first, but Buck blocked his path. The two dogs faced each other in a fight to the death. Spitz was an experienced, cunning fighter, but Buck used his superior intelligence and adaptability.

He feinted an attack at Spitz’s shoulder, then suddenly dove low, snapping Spitz’s leg bones. Defeated, Spitz fell into the snow, and Buck stood victorious as the undisputed leader.
