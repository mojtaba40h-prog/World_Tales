Chapter 4 of 7

Who Has Won to Mastership

Chapter summary

Morning brought the realization that Spitz was gone and Buck stood ready to take his place as lead dog. However, François initially chose Sol-leks, an experienced older dog, to lead the team. Buck refused to yield; he fiercely drove Sol-leks back and insisted on taking the lead position himself.

Recognizing Buck’s determination and fierce pride, François finally surrendered. Buck stepped into the lead harness, and the team immediately discovered that he was a master leader, far superior to Spitz. Buck restored strict discipline and order among the dogs with remarkable speed.

He punished troublemakers, encouraged weaker dogs, and kept the team moving at an unprecedented pace. Under his leadership, the team set record times, traveling across frozen trails in record days and arriving in Dawson City ahead of schedule. François and Perrault praised Buck’s exceptional intelligence and courage to everyone they met along the route.

Shortly after reaching their destination, François and Perrault received official orders that required them to sell the team. Buck was transferred to a “Scotch half-breed” mail carrier, who tasked the dogs with pulling immensely heavy loads of mail across thousands of miles. The work was exhausting and endless.

As the weeks dragged on, the dogs grew worn, thin, and weary. Dave, one of the oldest and most faithful dogs, became mortally ill but refused to be removed from his harness, dying bravely while fulfilling his duty to the trail.
