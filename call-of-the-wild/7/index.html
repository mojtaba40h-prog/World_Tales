The Sounding of the Call

Chapter summary

With the money won from the bet, Thornton paid off his debts and set off with his partners into the uncharted wilderness searching for a lost gold mine. Deep in the pristine forest, they discovered a rich valley stream brimming with gold dust. While the men labored happily, Buck had little to do.

A mysterious call began to echo through the forest—a haunting sound that filled Buck with wild longings and restless desires. Buck began venturing into the forest for days at a time. He befriended a wild timber wolf, running alongside him through the moonlit woods.

He hunted large game, killing a giant bull moose after a relentless multi-day pursuit. Yet, his deep love for Thornton always pulled him back to camp. However, upon returning from a hunt one day, Buck discovered a horrific scene: Yeehat indigenous raiders had attacked the camp, killing Thornton and his companions.

Filled with overwhelming grief and murderous fury, Buck attacked the Yeehats, driving them away in terror. With Thornton dead, the last tie binding Buck to the human world was severed forever. Soon after, a pack of wild wolves entered the valley.

After proving his strength by defeating their fiercest fighters, Buck was welcomed into the pack. He answered the call of his ancestors, becoming a legendary ghost dog who led the wolf pack through the wild northern wilderness.
