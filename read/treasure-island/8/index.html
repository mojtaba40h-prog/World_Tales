The Man in the Wilderness

Chapter summary

Fleeing deeper into the island’s wilderness, Jim encounters a wild, ragged man who introduces himself as Ben Gunn. Ben was marooned on the island three years earlier by Captain Flint’s pirate crew. Living on wild goats and berries, he has grown desperate for human company and normal food, particularly cheese.

Ben reveals that he knows about Flint’s buried treasure and has spent his years on the island exploring its secrets. Jim explains the current crisis on the Hispaniola and Silver’s violent mutiny. Realizing that Jim and the honest men need help, Ben offers his assistance in exchange for passage home and a fair share of the wealth.

He also mentions a small handmade boat he has built and hidden near a white rock by the shore.
