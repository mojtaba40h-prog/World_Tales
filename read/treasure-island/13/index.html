A Prisoner of the Pirates

Chapter summary

Returning to the stockade late at night, Jim slips inside, only to discover that the fort has been occupied by Silver and the remaining pirates. His friends have disappeared, leaving the station behind. Silver captures Jim but protects him from the angry crew, who want to execute the boy immediately.

The pirates present Silver with the "Black Spot," demanding new leadership, but Silver clever manipulation wins back their confidence. To Jim's immense surprise, Silver reveals that Dr. Livesey handed over the stockade, provisions, and the treasure map voluntarily. Jim wonders why his friends would surrender their strongest position and the map.

Silver keeps Jim close as a valuable hostage, realizing that his own survival now depends on maintaining a strategic truce with the boy.
