The Mysterious Sailor

Chapter summary

The story begins at the Admiral Benbow, a quiet coastal inn managed by young Jim Hawkins and his parents. One morning, an old, weather-beaten sailor named Billy Bones arrives and decides to stay. He carries a heavy wooden sea chest and keeps to himself, spending his days watching the sea through a brass telescope.

Billy drinks heavily, sings terrifying sea songs, and warns Jim to watch out for a "one-legged seafaring man." As weeks pass, Billy’s presence frightens the local patrons, but he refuses to leave or pay his bill. Jim’s father becomes gravely ill, adding stress to the household.

The mysterious sailor’s past remains completely unknown, yet his evident paranoia suggests he is running away from dangerous enemies. Jim grows increasingly curious about what secret contents are locked inside the sailor's mysterious wooden chest.
