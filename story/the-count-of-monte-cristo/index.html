The Count of Monte Cristo

by Alexandre Dumas

ClassicEnglish

In 1815 Marseille, the young sailor Edmond Dantès is betrayed by jealous rivals and imprisoned without trial in the Château d’If. Fourteen years later, after an escape and the discovery of a hidden fortune, he returns to France as the wealthy Count of Monte Cristo to settle his scores.
