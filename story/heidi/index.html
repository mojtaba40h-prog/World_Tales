Heidi

by Johanna Spyri

ClassicEnglish

Five-year-old Heidi is taken up the Alps to live with her gruff grandfather. Among the goats, flowers and snow she grows strong and happy, until a journey to Frankfurt and a friendship with Clara change both their lives.
