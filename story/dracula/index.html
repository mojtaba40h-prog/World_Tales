Dracula

by Bram Stoker

HorrorGothicEnglish

Jonathan Harker, a young English solicitor, travels to a ruined castle in the Carpathian Mountains to meet Count Dracula, and unknowingly sets an ancient evil loose on England. Professor Van Helsing and a band of hunters must follow the Count across Europe to destroy him.
